How you get a price

Every job is priced on the room it goes in, so there's no price list — you get a real figure, not a guess.

  1. You enquire

    Send your details through the form, or on WhatsApp. Measurements and a photo of the wall help, but they're not essential.

  2. We call you back

    We talk through what you're after and give you a price. Usually the same day, always within one working day.

  3. Consultation

    If it needs a proper look, we come out and measure up. Free within Dublin.

  4. Build and fit

    We build it and fit it, and tidy up after ourselves. Most jobs go in inside a day.
