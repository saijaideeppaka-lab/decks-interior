Studio

Carpenters first, designers too.

Decks Interior is a Dublin-based carpentry, interior design and construction studio working across Ireland. We design the space, make the joinery and fit it out ourselves — so the drawing that gets approved is the room that gets handed over.

Best known for bespoke media walls: slatted timber, marble and stone, with the fire, screen and lighting built in rather than added on.
